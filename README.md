## DSC 80 Pick2Win

Based on a competitive League of Legends matches dataset, we tried to predict game outcomes based on each teams' legend selection.
No statistically significant results were found, with only a weak difference between the win-rates of the most-picked characters and the least-picked. 

Data-cleaning involved identifying and filtering for the high missingness in the dataset, as seen in `data_cleaner.py`.
Final end-product notebook is viewable in `lyns-template.ipynb`, and was submitted alongside a video explaining our process.
